# Screening of the supplied research references

Reviewed 24 September 2026. The attachment contains **83 title/domain entries**, not 83 distinct primary studies. It omits article URLs and truncates many titles. This appendix screens every entry without implying that every paper received a full reading. Sources are linked only where identification was sufficiently confident. Unresolved entries are not evidence for implementation claims.

Levels: **M** = relevant methods/limitations inspected; **A** = primary abstract or metadata screened, not full replication/method review; **T** = supplied title/domain screened only; **U** = unresolved or ambiguous primary citation; **D** = duplicate; **N** = commentary, profile, index or discovery collection. A title-only exclusion is a scope decision, not an assessment of the paper's scientific quality.

Counts: M: 6, A: 31, T: 20, U: 6, D: 10, N: 10.
