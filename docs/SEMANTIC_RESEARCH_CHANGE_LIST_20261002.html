Research and proposed changes · 2 October 2026 · No inference code or defaults changed in this review

Revised recommendation: first audit and replace faulty routing, duplicated contracts and obsolete paths. Consolidate question ownership and actual model prompts. Then test a restricted comparison evaluator and balanced demonstrations within the existing stages. No additional recovery layers, sample-specific fixes or accuracy claims from routing checks. See the revised P0–P7 sequence below.

This focused review checks nine relevant papers, prompt/method appendices and selected author implementation files. It is not an exhaustive systematic review. The Hugging Face connector failed; public HF metadata/full markdown worked for several papers, with original arXiv pages as fallback. The DynaDebate repository was unavailable, so its implementation claims here are paper-level only.

What our latest evidence actually says

The fixed four-case paired trial ended 1/4 correct in both focused and reason-first variants. Each made one useful correction and one harmful revision against the recorded labels. Correct proposals increased from one to two, without an additional correct final explanation. Shorter isolated-stage contracts improved label counts but generated unsupported conclusions. These are selected development results, not held-out accuracy estimates.

Correct OCR survives in the bottle case, but a wrong comparison is repeatedly approved. The bubble-wrap case has contradictory model-generated statuses. The idiom succeeds in the full tribunal despite failing in isolated verification. The jersey case accepts originality without comparison evidence. These distinguish semantic comparison, task interpretation, output consistency and verification failures; they do not prove a universal model-capacity ceiling.

Local evidence: latest controlled report, previous condition iteration, causal qualification record.

Faithful Chain-of-Thought — 2301.13379The model translates a problem into a symbolic representation; a deterministic solver computes the result. Correct execution does not guarantee correct translation.Test a restricted comparison evaluator for explicit quantities, order, negation and comparable outcomes. Verify extraction separately. Debate or Vote — 2508.17536v1Separates initial-answer voting from peer debate across seven NLP benchmarks, using five agents in its main comparison. Much of the reported benefit comes from aggregation. The martingale claim belongs to the paper’s formal model, not a universal impossibility theorem.Compare debate against independent assessment at matched resources. Evaluate whether agents contribute distinct correct evidence, not just whether they agree. No oracle-based selection or confidence-weighted automatic acceptance.
DynaDebate — 2601.05746v2, preprintAllocates distinct reasoning paths, critiques specific steps, and triggers tools on disagreement. Main configuration: three agents, two rounds; GPT-4o-mini, Qwen3-8B and Llama-3.3-70B. The paper includes tool-equipped single-agent controls. Its linked code repository returned 404 during this review.Borrow concrete inference critiques, not the full architecture. Do not copy stopping on unanimous agreement: our bottle case is unanimously wrong. Tools can resolve an observable fact or formal comparison, not certify arbitrary pragmatic intent.
The Cost of Consensus — 2605.00914v1, preprintCompares homogeneous peer debate, isolated revision and unrelated-peer-context controls with 7–8B models. It reports context-induced error propagation and uses task-specific token budgets; 350 tokens caused serious format problems on its harder MMLU setting, leading to 1,024.Separate reliability from reasoning gains. Add peer-free controls and fixed-seed stability tests. The study concerns unguided debate on math/knowledge tasks, not a proof that our evidence-based tribunal must fail.
Reasoning Beyond Literal — 2601.17197v1, preprintUses a 90B vision teacher and Qwen2.5-VL-3B student, distilled reasoning SFT, then GRPO. Experiments use two H100 GPUs and figurative-style classification datasets. Reward checks answer correctness and format, not every explanation step.If controlled prompting fails, evaluate supervised semantic training separately. Its gains cannot be credited to a prompt template or transferred directly to V-FLUTE. Training must reward/review grounded explanations, not labels alone.
V-FLUTE — 2405.01474v2 / NAACL 2025Defines explainable figurative visual entailment. Sarcasm construction differs from generic speaker-intent classification. ExplanationScore averages BERTScore and BLEURT; specified backbones are DeBERTa-xlarge-MNLI and BLEURT-20. References include model generation plus human editing and are not infallible.Retain the official labels and task convention, flag disputed references separately, and assess image faithfulness plus the decisive inference. Similar wording cannot establish correct polarity or roles.

These papers do not establish a magic JSON shape or universal answer length. Their gains depend on task, model, demonstrations, training, tools, and grading. Some score final answers or factual statements rather than every explanatory inference. Stability must be measured under the actual configuration, not inferred from the method name.

Revised priority: replace faulty structure before semantic experiments

User constraints incorporated: repair, remove or replace the faulty mechanism itself. Do not keep stacking recovery paths or new judges. No production rule may depend on a sample ID, image hash, caption match, reference answer or expected label. Provenance identity checks and named regression fixtures remain legitimate. Changes must target a general failure mechanism and be evaluated across the intended V-FLUTE population. Dataset-wide applicability is a design requirement; improvement across the dataset remains an empirical question.

Status: code inspection plus eight small deterministic routing probes; no GPU inference and no production edits. This is a focused structural audit, not a completed whole-repository reachability certification. The earlier R1–R6 research candidates remain below as supporting detail; the sequence in this section supersedes their implementation order. Existing unrelated working-tree changes were left intact.

Revised implementation sequence and acceptance checks

  1. P0 — Trace before deletion. Inventory CLI, stagewise runner, witnesses, proposer, verification and final gate. For each path record callers, enabling configuration, inputs, actual model prompt/schema, budget, output consumers and terminal status. Count fallback use and non-dispatched questions from existing records; use bounded stubbed dispatch checks where records are insufficient. Separate intentional abstention from parser, timeout, missing-field and configuration failures. Include a retain/replace/remove disposition and replacement owner for every changed branch. Static import absence alone is not proof of dead code.
  2. P1 — Replace question selection and delivery defects. Correct the precedence and source-routing defects above. Consolidate selection, validation and delivery around the existing question record. No hidden generic substitution. Choose the missing decisive obligation from current evidence; if the evidence already answers it, proceed to interpretation without asking again. A claim requiring unavailable history remains unresolved. Test invalid inputs, all supported request origins, paraphrases, negative instructions and reordered fields.
  3. P2 — Rewrite the actual question and response contracts. Review initial observation, caption testimony, evidence selection, role mapping, relation and audit prompts. Keep only fields with an identified consumer. Prefer a neutral open factual answer when alternatives would lead the witness; use a compact closed answer only when genuinely appropriate. Give relational questions both entities and panel/speaker bindings. Use grounded figurative interpretations in the semantic stage without asking pixel witnesses to prove an abstract property. Keep prompt/schema/token budgets aligned; do not force arbitrary short prose that loses qualifiers.
  4. P3 — Simplify judgment and gate ownership. Reuse one canonical condition assessment and derive redundant summaries. The model evaluates context-dependent meaning; code enforces evidence identity, completeness, status consistency and permitted transitions. Retain rejection when a material condition is unknown. Replace the faulty projection or schema instead of repairing contradictions through an extra model call. Inspect flag consumers before renaming; preserve honest legacy readers.
  5. P4 — Replace supported free-text comparison with checked execution. Trial the R2 evaluator inside the existing relation stage for explicitly supported comparisons. It replaces that computation rather than adds a second reviewer. Preserve role, time, units, negation and intention/outcome distinctions; require validated operands. Unsupported figurative cases use the declared semantic path, not a numerical guess. Train-only balanced examples from R3 are a separate ablation, not bundled into this change.
  6. P5 — Consolidate interaction and remove redundant recovery. Reuse the independent assessment and final audit to check the decisive inferential step. Allow at most the existing bounded follow-up when a named, answerable missing fact can materially affect the decision. Otherwise stop with recorded uncertainty. Keep useful transport/completion protections, but remove duplicate retries or repairs whose outputs cannot affect the maintained path. No extra judge, debate round or generic self-reflection path.
  7. P6 — Retire unused implementation after replacement is qualified. Remove proven unreachable branches and unused generated fields; isolate necessary archived replay adapters. Keep one maintained production route and explicitly named evaluation controls. Preserve historical results and reproducibility; fail clearly on retired runtime modes or migrate them explicitly. Do not delete a module just because its experiment did not improve accuracy. Audit checkpoints, cache identities and resume behavior before changing interfaces.
  8. P7 — Validate structure, semantics and stability separately. First prove corrected routing/dispatch with deterministic and bounded real-path checks. Then compare each semantic change alone on a frozen image-group-disjoint development cohort covering the target phenomena, labels, literal comparisons and uncertainty. Use checked semantic flips and paraphrases, not caption-specific production rules. Report joint label/explanation correctness, harmful revisions, wrong confirmations, false rejections, unanswered conditions, first-attempt completion, fallback rate, retries, calls/tokens and latency. Repeat a small subset across seeds, order and resume. Keep feedback disabled, references out of inference, and full dataset execution user-controlled. A lower call count is an efficiency gain; it is not by itself an accuracy gain.

Question design grounded in the papers

CoVe supports separating the factual check from the proposed answer. For this project, ask “What duration is stated for each labeled outcome?” rather than “Does this prove the first outcome is faster?” Woodpecker motivates concrete visual targets and keeping full-image context for relationships. DSG motivates qualifying question coverage and dependencies; a question about an absent prerequisite should not produce a fabricated attribute. These are transferable mechanisms, not evidence that their exact prompt or token limit is optimal for our models.

For semantic checking, a question such as “Which depicted property licenses the caption’s figurative comparison, and which required part remains unsupported?” belongs to the existing semantic stage. It must permit UNRESOLVED and cannot imply that a mapping exists. For auditing, request the specific unsupported premise-to-conclusion step and evidence; do not demand an objection when the conclusion is sound. Keep a compact answer contract with a source-bound observation or interpretation and explicit uncertainty, replacing redundant fields rather than accumulating them.

Completion criteria: every retained branch has a current purpose; every removed branch has caller/replay checks; all question origins share the same capability policy; requested versus effective configuration is visible; no silent generic question substitutes for a failed request; no increase in the permitted recovery budget; semantic promotion still requires grounded explanation gains without unexplained harm. Initial-arbiter redesign remains outside the existing scope; inspecting its interfaces is included. No claim of whole-dataset improvement will be made from the structural probes.

Supporting research candidates (R1–R6)

The items below are proposed FigDebate adaptations, not experimentally established improvements. R1, R2 and R3 should initially be separate arms against the current focused baseline. Do not combine them until their individual effects are known. R4 is a prerequisite for using automatic decomposition, not permission to bypass its current safety fallback. R5 must earn its extra interaction, if any. R6 is conditional.

R1 — Isolate canonical judgment consistency

Owners: engine/evidence_review_v5.py; engine/condition_review.py; engine/output_contracts.py

Reuse the existing canonical-condition projection in a small experimental variant of the focused path. Generate one status per checked condition and derive redundant relation/unresolved lists. Keep uncertainty and evidence checks. Do not automatically delete contradictory fields after generation.

Difference from previous work: Already partially implemented in condition-audit-1, which did not qualify. This is a controlled component ablation, not a new semantic solution. Do not bring along the larger state-pair schema or other condition-mode changes.

Qualification: Scripted contradictions must remain blocked; live contradiction frequency must fall without losing the valid idiom correction or accepting weak evidence. Report reliability gain separately from accuracy.

R2 — Test a restricted, executable comparison

Owners: engine/condition_review.py; engine/evidence_review_v5.py; proposed engine/relation_constraints.py

In shadow mode, map explicit comparison evidence to typed operands with subject, speaker, actual-versus-intended status, comparable quantity/order and source IDs. For representable cases, compute the relationship with a fixed interpreter. Test extraction and calculation separately, then reuse the existing relation slot to verify source bindings. Unsupported semantics return UNRESOLVED, not a guessed value.

Difference from previous work: NEW: previous state_pairs were free-text actual/asserted strings followed by a model-chosen assessment. Correctly copying those strings did not force a correct comparison. This proposal computes only the formal part and retains a fallible, separately checked translation boundary.

Qualification: First use evaluation-only checked operands to verify the mechanism, then automatically extracted operands on unseen development cases. Include role swaps, wishes versus outcomes, negation, units and true missing evidence. No sample-specific dictionaries or unconditional quick=week/month=slow rule.

R3 — Teach the task convention with balanced contrast examples

Owners: engine/semantic_protocol.py; engine/simple_judge.py; engine/tribunal_interpretation.py

Test a small fixed bank of reviewed training-only examples demonstrating observation, licensed interpretation and comparison. Pair valid figurative support with tempting but unsupported mappings; show how sarcastic praise differs from agreement with criticism. Preserve the full source claim. Fit examples within the measured context budget by replacing repetitive instructions, not truncating sources.

Difference from previous work: NEW ablation of demonstrations versus instruction-only prompts. Our existing rules already state these principles but are not reliably followed. An explicit interpretation field is not new and must not be credited as a fresh mechanism.

Qualification: Test the same examples in several fixed orders and on image-group-disjoint validation. Evaluate preservation of qualifiers, figurative-property correspondence and wrong-evidence acceptance. Never place these diagnostic images, their references, or their near-duplicates in the demonstration bank.

R4 — Qualify focused questions and decomposition before activation

Owners: engine/claim_graph.py; engine/source_spans.py; agents/visual_adapter.py; engine/evidence_review_v5.py

Retain the current full-caption fallback. Evaluate a small number of source-linked subclaims/questions for coverage, scope, negation and actual/desired distinctions. Ask an open factual question only when a decisive fact is genuinely missing; keep the proposed verdict and preferred answer out of that call. Reuse the current witness allowance.

Difference from previous work: The DSG-inspired graph and fresh verifier contexts already exist. Automatic decomposition failed and is deliberately unqualified. Do not turn it on merely because a paper used graphs. Correct bottle OCR means more OCR questions are not the first remedy there.

Qualification: Compare extracted obligations with reviewed annotations before measuring answer labels. A valid source span does not prove semantic coverage. Unreadable evidence stays unknown; absent literal wings must not invalidate a benevolence metaphor.

R5 — Make the audit locate the decisive unsupported inference

Owners: engine/evidence_review_v5.py; engine/tribunal_repair.py; engine/review_routing.py

Within the existing independent relation/challenge stages, separate two responsibilities: establish decisive facts without seeing the proposed answer, then identify the first premise-to-conclusion step that is unsupported. A dispute names the exact source clause, evidence and missing or opposing relation. Repair once only when the required check can supply new information; an unchanged opinion is not progress.

Difference from previous work: Existing focused objections already name defects; the new experiment must target a specific transition rather than add more generic critique instructions. Fresh context, bounded repair and exact audited explanation binding are already present. Preserve them.

Qualification: Measure false verification of wrong proposals, including same-label confirmations, and false rejection of sound explanations. Include examples where all agents initially agree wrongly. Compare against a peer-free relation assessment with the same evidence and allowance.

R6 — Capability and training decision, only if simpler changes fail

Owners: evaluation/qualify_causal_relation.py; evaluation/qualify_grounded_gate.py; future training configuration

Run a small matched verifier comparison using another already available suitable model, or a separately budgeted model only after feasibility is established. If checked facts and qualified claim representations still yield wrong relations across prompt variants, consider supervised training on reviewed evidence-to-claim judgments and counterexamples.

Difference from previous work: A controlled capability probe, not a claim that model size explains the failures. Existing agents or another Qwen variant do not automatically supply independent errors. Training is separate from prompt/interface ablations and requires a reviewed dataset and untouched test split.

Qualification: Compare paired errors and accepted label-plus-explanation quality, not standalone model accuracy alone. Establish hardware feasibility separately; the two-H100 training recipe is not an 8GB laptop prescription. Do not train on the evaluation packet or reward correct labels with faulty explanations.

The proposed comparator must distinguish the actual outcomes from intentions and preserve both participants. For a qualified relative-duration representation, opposite orders can be calculated rather than redescribed as support. Source-to-representation translation remains fallible. This example is a mechanism illustration and regression, never a special production rule or a prompt demonstration drawn from the evaluation packet.

For “doctor as an angel,” the task is different: establish a licensed benevolence/care interpretation and whether the depicted action supports it. A detector cannot verify kindness by finding wings. For originality, appearance alone is not comparative evidence. The two cases must not share a simplistic rule that all metaphors are allowed or all abstract claims are unknowable.

Preserve and defer

Preserve V-FLUTE, local model loading, working OCR, source identity, evidence provenance, checkpoint/resume, completion recovery, bounded case/repair budgets and feedback disabled. Leave the initial arbiter outside this tribunal-focused revision. Do not turn on reason-first or condition modes by default. Do not add more judges, mandatory boxes for abstract concepts, automatic polarity reversal for sarcasm, reference retrieval at inference, uncalibrated confidence voting, or hidden retries until the desired answer appears.

For reproducibility, keep model revisions and prompt/schema versions pinned, key caches by every relevant input, retain raw attempts and termination reasons, and test that restarts/resume reproduce the same effective configuration. These controls mostly exist; audit them rather than rebuilding the runtime.
